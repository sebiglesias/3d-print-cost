Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate the weight of my 3D print?

Slice your 3D model in your slicer software (Bambu Studio, Cura, or PrusaSlicer). The slicer will report the estimated filament weight in grams (g), including infill, perimeters, and supports.

What is machine depreciation in 3D printing?

A $600 3D printer typically has a productive lifespan of 1,500 to 2,000 printing hours before major overhaul. Allocating $0.30 to $0.50 per hour covers nozzle replacements, grease, PTFE tubes, and long-term hardware replacement.
